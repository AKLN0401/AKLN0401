### Khoi Nguyen

I'm interested in what happens between a device on a wafer and a working hardware system, which is why my work spans both cleanroom fabrication and digital hardware design.

- ASIC Design / Research Internship, University of Waterloo — CMOS Design and Reliability Group — 12-nm FinFET, static timing analysis, DFT, MMMC, synthesis, physical design
